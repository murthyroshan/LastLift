## Seven years of closures, replayed through the graph

MBTA keeps no lift-outage history of its own, but MassDOT publishes every service alert, and MBTA's LAMP archive goes back to 2019. LastLift replays every reported elevator closure since 1 January 2024 through today's step-free graph ([`validation/HISTORY.md`](../validation/HISTORY.md)). One archive artifact is excluded by a written rule: <!--claim:hist_lamp_artifact_windows-->104<!--/claim--> LAMP-only closures that all start in the same minute (22 February 2025, 18:33 UTC) and appear nowhere in the portal; the before-and-after numbers are in the errata there:

- **<!--claim:hist_dirs_lost-->251<!--/claim--> platform-directions lost step-free access at least once**, for <!--claim:hist_lost_service_h-->55,066<!--/claim--> platform-direction hours within scheduled service; <!--claim:hist_single_share-->95%<!--/claim--> of those hours trace to a single lift. Redundancy was defeated (two or more lifts out together) <!--claim:hist_concurrent_losses-->310<!--/claim--> times.
- The costliest single lift was <!--claim:hist_top_lift-->811<!--/claim--> (<!--claim:hist_top_lift_service_h-->6,866<!--/claim--> service hours). Each lift's availability, closures and median closure length are in the graph (`(:Outage)-[:OF]->(:Lift)`) and in the agent's `lift_history` tool.
- **The certified k explains real exposure:** single-lift platform-directions lost access in <!--claim:hist_bt_k1_month_share-->46%<!--/claim--> of months, those with two or more lift routes in <!--claim:hist_bt_k2_month_share-->4%<!--/claim-->.
- **What history cannot do (a negative result we publish):** ranking lifts by their 2024 record does not pick out the lifts that cost the most in 2025 (Spearman <!--claim:hist_bt_spearman-->0.157<!--/claim--> over <!--claim:hist_bt_lifts-->69<!--/claim--> lifts, precision of the top ten <!--claim:hist_bt_p10-->0.1<!--/claim-->), and a monthly closure model fails the calibration bar set in advance (Brier skill <!--claim:hist_bt_brier_skill-->0.089<!--/claim-->). So LastLift shows historical frequencies and never a failure forecast.
- With each lift's own outage rate instead of a uniform guess, <!--claim:rel_emp_dirs_over_5pct-->20<!--/claim--> platform-directions would be without step-free access more than 5% of the time.
- Checks: 20 random moments of the replay re-checked with FalkorDB's certified max-flow on a copy of the graph; totals reproduce from the committed files in the tests; a sensitivity table (imputed ends in or out, episode gaps of 0, 30 and 120 minutes, calendar or service hours); the 2025 total from the portal alone reproduces the planning probe within 1%, and the sources are compared side by side. The portal's timestamps turned out to be Boston local time labelled as UTC; the history reads them as local time.
