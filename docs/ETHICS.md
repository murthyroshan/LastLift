# Ethics: people, language and consent

LastLift is about riders who need step-free access: wheelchair users, people with mobility disabilities, people with strollers, older riders, people with luggage. This page says how the project treats their experience and how it talks about them.

## Not yet reviewed by disabled riders

LastLift has **not yet been reviewed by disabled riders**. It is designed for that review. Until then, nothing in this repository, the website or the video presents itself as reflecting riders' experience.

## No invented lived experience

- No invented quotes, named personas or "a wheelchair user told us". If a scenario is ever illustrated, it carries the line "Illustrative scenario, not a research participant."
- Only published, attributable public material is cited, attributed to the organisation that published it, with its URL and date (for example MBTA's own pages, public advisory-committee minutes, settlement press releases).
- An organisation's published statement is never re-voiced as an individual's testimony.
- The video avoids inspiration framing.

## If someone with lived experience reviews the work

1. A short written consent note first: the purpose, what is recorded, how any quote is used, their right to review and withdraw, and their choice of name, initials or anonymity.
2. Only text they have approved is quoted, and credit is offered.
3. No filming without separate consent.
4. Any compensation is disclosed, or the fact that there was none.

## Language

- "Riders who need step-free access" is the default. "Disabled riders" is used when quoting advocacy sources.
- Results describe the open-data model: a platform "loses step-free access in the open-data model". LastLift never calls a person stranded or a route safe, never calls a station accessible or inaccessible outright, and says "cannot assess" where the published data is missing. <!-- copy-lint: quoted -->
- LastLift never attributes its own output to the MBTA, never predicts lift failures, never estimates repair times, and makes no legal or compliance claims. <!-- copy-lint: quoted -->
- When the open data and MBTA's own advice differ, LastLift shows both and tells riders to follow MBTA's advice on site.

## Feedback

Anyone can [report an accessibility problem with LastLift](../../../issues/new?template=accessibility-problem.yml). The form is in plain language and every field is optional. It is public, so anyone who would rather not post publicly (for example, about their own disability) can email mrslvt.dev@gmail.com instead. Reports are read by the author and are never used as quotes without the consent steps above.
