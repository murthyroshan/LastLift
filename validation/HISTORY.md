# Lift Ledger: closure history replayed through the step-free graph

Generated by `scripts/history_report.py` from `data/history/` (built by `ingest/history.py`, `scripts/history_replay.py`, `scripts/backtest.py`). Every number here is reproducible from committed files plus the four public archives below.

**What these numbers are.** MBTA alerts record *reported* closures; MBTA may post or close an alert late. Today's station layouts are applied to past closures, so lifts replaced or added since 2024 are listed under topology drift. Nothing here is a forecast.

**Archive artifact (errata, 2026-10-18).** Rule: 50+ LAMP-only windows starting in the same minute, none of them in the portal, are dropped as an archive artifact. It removes 104 windows, all starting at 2025-02-22T18:33Z: each a different one-lift alert, absent from the portal (where 5 closures start that day), and nearly all ending the next day. The largest real event in either source is 47 lifts under one alert, confirmed by both. Before the rule, the ledger read 285 platform-directions, 58,286 lost service hours, a 92.9% single-lift share and 390 concurrent losses; the numbers below are after it. ## Historical gold (H7)

20 archived closures (seed in `eval/sampling.json`) are labelled blind by the author from MBTA's own text, without seeing any model output (`validation/gold_history_author.csv`; ids and text hashes in `validation/history_gold_ids.csv`), then compared with the model's verdict for the lifts closed at that moment (`scripts/history_gold.py --score`).
