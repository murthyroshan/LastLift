# Lift Ledger: closure history replayed through the step-free graph

Generated by `scripts/history_report.py` from `data/history/` (built by `ingest/history.py`, `scripts/history_replay.py`, `scripts/backtest.py`). Every number here is reproducible from committed files plus the four public archives below.

**What these numbers are.** MBTA alerts record *reported* closures; MBTA may post or close an alert late. Today's station layouts are applied to past closures, so lifts replaced or added since 2024 are listed under topology drift. Nothing here is a forecast.
